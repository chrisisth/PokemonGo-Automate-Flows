This flow is designed to continually re-add you to the raid lobby before the countdown expires.
The idea is to make your presence in the raid visible to others in the hope that they will join.

1. **Start**: Press the volume down button to activate the flow. It'll show a short message

2. **Operation**: The flow will automatically exit and reenter you to the raid lobby before the countdown expires.

3. **End**: You can stop the flow at any time by pressing the volume up button. To kill the flow, you need to manually stop it in the Automate app.

### Features

- **Cancel**: You can stop the flow at any time by pressing the volume up button.
- **Restart**: If you wish to restart the flow, simply press the volume down button again.

This flow is designed to work with the free version of the Automate app by LlamaLab.
